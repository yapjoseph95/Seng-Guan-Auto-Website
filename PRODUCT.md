# Product

## Platform

Web. The existing website is a static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site.

## Users

General used-car buyers in Malaysia comparing vehicle options, considering financing, and deciding whether to visit or contact the dealership. The user confirmed this broad audience for the prototype round.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors browse Seng Guan Auto's featured vehicles, understand the available buying and financing help, and take a clear next step by checking current listings, calling, emailing, or opening WhatsApp.

## Positioning

Seng Guan Auto Sdn. Bhd. is presented by the current website as a used-car dealership in Banting, Selangor. The current About page says the business was established in 1995; retain that as existing copy only until the owner confirms it for the redesigned site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The redesign must not require a backend, store enquiry data, or send a form to a server.
- The loan estimate and any inventory filters in prototypes run entirely in the browser.
- Current vehicle availability, pricing, and mileage belong on the linked Carlist dealer page; do not invent or treat old sample listings as live stock.
- Contact actions may open the existing phone, email, and WhatsApp destinations.
- Use the existing Seng Guan logo and vehicle photography where they fit the selected direction.
- The source website contains claims about inspection, warranties, financing rates, approval times, and customer testimonials. Treat these as copy needing owner confirmation; do not strengthen them or add unsupported numbers or guarantees.
- Keep the English-language content and the local Banting address in these prototypes.
